ABOUT

Make the company feel established without making the site feel old.

TAnfu E-Seal Technology Company Limited is a professional manufacturer of high-security sealing solutions based in China. Formerly known as Suzhou Skyland Hardware Manufacturing Co., Ltd., the company was originally established in 2009 and has over 15 years of manufacturing experience in the security seal industry

The company operates approximately 15 production lines with a workforce of around 60 skilled employees, covering a production area of 5,000 square meters. Our monthly production capacity reaches up to 5 million pieces. We are certified under ISO 9001 quality management standards, ensuring consistent product quality and operational excellence.
